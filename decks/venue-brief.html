Section C / What we bring

Talent, production and promotion, already sourced.

Sound

Klarity Audio. Full system, run setup to teardown by their own engineer. Scales from a single stage to a two stage build.

Lights and visuals

Projection mapping, lasers and DMX. Our lead visuals operator ran mainstage lasers for Marshmello and builds his own mapping software and GrandMA3 plugins. Full 360 interior and exterior mapping.

Talent

A standing roster of twenty two Phoenix artists plus direct agency lanes for national touring acts. We book, contract and pay talent ourselves.

Promotion

Paid social at a measured $0.163 per click, an owned buyer list, and artist cross promotion across the full bill. We fund our own marketing.

Vendors

A curated vendor program where vendors keep one hundred percent of their sales. No cut taken. It fills the site and it is why they come back.

Compliance

Permit filing, site plans, insurance, licensing, security staffing and sanitation ratios. We do this work before we ask you for a date.

Supplier contracts

Every critical supplier now signs a failure to perform clause before a dollar moves, with a named backup and a payment schedule that keeps their balance above their cost of walking. We wrote it after a supplier cost us a show, and it is the reason that cannot happen in your building.

Section D / Deal structure

We work whichever way your room works.

We have no preference between these and no ego about it. Tell us which one you run and we will price to it.

Flat buyout

We rent the room outright, sell our own tickets, bring our own security and production, and carry the risk.

Full service house

You run ticketing, box office and security. We bring the talent and the promotion and settle against your statement.

Door split

An agreed split against a defined line, written down before doors, settled the same night.

Your property, our permit

For open lots and non traditional sites we file the permit, bind the insurance naming you, and hand you the approval letter before anything is built.

Section E / What we need from you

Five answers and we can price a date.

  1. Which dates are genuinely open, and what a hold costs.
  2. Real capacity, and whether that number is people or vehicles.
  3. Rental, and what is included against what is billed back.
  4. Who sells the tickets and who provides security.
  5. Sound curfew, age policy, alcohol policy and available power.

We are not asking whether you would allow a show like this.

We are asking which date. Everything on the compliance side is already built and already proven, and we would rather send you a permit number than a pitch.
