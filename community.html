What this is

Everyone's in

Bedroom producer with zero plays or headliner with a decade in — same room, same respect. There's no velvet rope here. The underground is the door being open.

Big teaches small

The ones who've been at it hand it down — mixing, booking, visuals, surviving your first gig. The Circle is where that happens. The small keep the big honest.

Scared is the ticket in

Never posted your art? Never played out? Never told anyone you make stuff? That feeling is exactly why this exists. Come as you are. Express yourself. <3

How it works

  1. Walk in. Add yourself to The Fam — name, what you make, where you're at. Every level counts as a level.
  2. Say something. Drop your first post on The Floor — a track, a sketch, a question, a win, or just how it's going. Anonymous is allowed.
  3. Link up. Hit The Circle to learn from the vets or pass down what you know. That's how a scene gets built.

House rules

  • Hype over hate. Critique when it's asked for, love always.
  • Every level belongs. "Beginner" is a stage, not a rank.
  • No gatekeeping. Share the plug, the technique, the stage.
  • What's shared here stays supported. Someone opening up is trust — treat it that way.
  • Family first. We build the underground together or not at all.
