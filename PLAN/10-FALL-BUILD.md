Bringing them in on a small, low-pressure night is exactly how that relationship should start, and it gives them a reference before a $68K weekend rides on them.

⚠️ **Drone reality check.** Commercial drone operation requires an **FAA Part 107 certificate** — that is the operator's licence, not a formality, and "my friend has a drone" does not satisfy it if the footage is used commercially. Indoor flight avoids airspace authorization but not the certificate. **Ask any drone candidate for their Part 107 number before booking.** Also confirm the Ice House permits indoor flight at all; many venues do not.

**Content spec to send every artist in advance** — this is the highest-leverage thing on the whole night, and the lesson is already logged: the fix is the spec to artists, not more cropping afterward. **Native portrait 1080×1920, 30fps.** CHIRENJI's submission is the correct example to cite.

**Success measure:** reels delivered per artist, not dollars. RAW inside 48 hours, curated over two weeks.

## 4 · DRIVE-IN — two stages

**West Wind Glendale 9 · 5650 N 55th Ave, Glendale · 623-939-9715 · westwinddi.com/events/host-your-event · 50-car minimum.**

**Subtronics has already played this venue.** Also Willie Nelson and KC and the Sunshine Band. They run car shows with vendors — the exact format SubGround just delivered under a city permit. The pitch is not "will you allow a bass show," it is "which Saturday."

### Two things the research changes about Ryan's plan

**① Nine screens is why two stages actually works.** The venue has **nine separate screens**, which means two stages can occupy two different screen fields with natural separation instead of fighting each other for sightlines and sound. And the projection-mapping idea is not speculative here — **the screens already exist and are already the venue's whole architecture.** This is the rare case where the production concept is cheaper at the bigger venue.

**② The 2,000 capacity number needs verifying, and it may not mean what it looks like.** A drive-in's capacity is counted in **cars**, not people. 2,000 cars and 2,000 people are wildly different events with different ticketing, different porta-potty math and different security. **Confirm on the call: capacity in cars AND in people, whether tickets are per-car or per-person, and whether SubGround sells the tickets or buys the room flat.** Every number in the money model assumed 2,500 people and that assumption is unverified.
