## 0. THE FIVE RULES THIS ENGINE RUNS ON

1. **Never name a number first.** Ask for their best number. Every time you speak first you pay for it.
2. **Stack non-cash value before you talk cash.** The stack is free to SubGround and expensive to replace. It comes in the same breath as the ask so their number arrives already discounted.
3. **The roster is the floor.** Every bill is built on the 22 roster artists first. You buy the top of the bill only.
4. **Every event funds the next event's deposits** (Pipeline Law 1). A booking you cannot fund from money already in hand is not a booking, it is a wish.
5. **Never let an artist go unpaid or unlisted to save a dollar** (`FACTS.md` §15). This engine is built to pay less, not to pay late. Law 4 says today's $500 vendors are DREAM's department heads. The same is true of artists.

**And the standing prohibition:** never claim a capacity, headcount, or stat that is not real. Ryan already refused to tell a vendor "1,200 guaranteed." Use the real proof instead. It is better proof anyway.

## 1. THE STANDING BOOKING STRUCTURE

This is Ryan's, not Claude's. Reproduced as it works.

### 1.1 The move

> **Never name a number first. Ask for THEIR best number. Stack non-cash value in the same message so the number comes in low.**

The sequence in one breath:

1. Open with **proof**, not with a pitch.
2. Ask availability for a specific date.
3. Lay out the **non-cash stack** as things that come *on top of* the fee.
4. Ask: **"Send me your best number for that date and what it covers."**
5. Say when you will come back. Then actually come back.

You do not counter in the first reply. You take the number, sit on it for a beat, and come back with either a yes or one structured counter that trades cash for stack.

### 1.2 The stack — four items, all free to SubGround

| # | What they get | What it actually is | What it costs SubGround |

All 25% off. | The discount, plus whatever cut Ryan sets. See 1.3. |

- **IG DMs cap at 1,000 characters and silently refuse at the limit.** A 1,000-character draft never sends and throws no error. Keep artist DMs short or move to email.

### 2.3 Buy only the top of the bill

The shape of every ticketed show on the slate:

```
  1 bought headliner        <- the only line item with a fee
  1 to 2 bought direct support  <- only if the date justifies it
  6 to 10 roster artists    <- $0, the whole middle and bottom of the bill
```

**Rules for the roster layer:**
- Roster slots are **15-minute back-to-back sets** on content nights (the Icehouse format) and full sets on ticketed nights. Short sets mean more artists on the flyer, more reposts, more codes in circulation.
- Every roster artist on a bill gets **a code, a tag and the footage** (per §1.2). That is the payment. Honor it or the floor stops being free.
- **Never cut a roster artist to make room for a bought name after the flyer is out.** Adding a name is fine, removing one is how you lose the floor.
- Roster support is locked at **T-14** on the calendar in §8, after the headliner is announced, so the headliner announcement gets a clean day.
