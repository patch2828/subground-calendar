# MODEL CROSS-CHECK — and the correction it produced

**Status as of 2026-08-26: this document's original numbers were WRONG and are superseded. `04-MONEY.md` is the canonical money model for this repo.** What follows is the reconciliation, kept rather than deleted because the disagreement is itself useful information.

---

## What happened

Two independent models of the same six months were built. They disagreed by roughly $99,000 on the conservative case, which is not a rounding difference — it is two different businesses.

| | This doc (original) | `04-MONEY.md` |
|---|---:|---:|
| CONSERVATIVE | +$45,237 | **−$53,793** |
| TARGET | +$110,153 | **+$41,317** |
| SELLOUT | +$204,768 | **+$136,250** |
| $100K needs | ~70% fill | **~89% fill** |

**`04-MONEY.md` is right and this doc was optimistic.** The reason is entirely costs, and it is not close:

| Show | This doc | 04-MONEY | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 01 Gold Rush | $9,779 | $13,544 | +$3,765 |
| 03 Halloween | $8,900 | $14,253 | +$5,353 |
| 04 Combine × NR | $9,400 | $14,443 | +$5,043 |
| 05 TH3 N3TW0RK | $4,200 | $9,227 | +$5,027 |
| 06 NYE | $10,500 | $17,423 | +$6,923 |
| 07 January | $9,000 | $15,373 | +$6,373 |
| 08 February | $9,200 | $15,373 | +$6,173 |
| 09 Drive-In | $26,000 | $39,464 | +$13,464 |
| **Total** | **$86,979** | **$139,100** | **+$52,121** |

This document used round per-show estimates. `04-MONEY.md` built line-item costs against `FACTS.md` for every show, and it revised even Gold Rush **upward** from the $9,779 in FACTS to $13,544 — the one show whose costs are best known. A model that costs the known show higher than the fact base, and shows its work, beats a model that rounds.

**Lesson worth keeping: when two models disagree, the one with line items wins over the one with round numbers. Always.**

## THE CANONICAL NUMBERS

| Scenario | Six-month net | Against $100K |
|---|---:|---|
| CONSERVATIVE (~40% fill) | **−$53,793** | Every single show loses money. The business does not survive this case. |
| TARGET (~70% fill) | **+$41,317** | Short by $58,683 |
| SELLOUT | **+$136,250** | Clears it by $36,250 |

**$100,000 requires roughly 89% average sell-through across all nine shows**, or target sell-through plus about **$58,683 of sponsorship** — a layer currently at $0 booked and $0 pitched.

### The conservative case is not "a smaller win"

At conservative fill **every one of the nine shows loses money**, including Gold Rush. That is not a slow year, it is a failure mode, and it means the downside is not "we make less" — it is "we run out of cash." Nothing in the plan should be read as if the floor is zero.

## What this does to the chain — the biggest change

Phase 2, On The Rocks, needs **$78,196 to $83,196** (the $68,196 ledger plus its $10–15K deposit reserve).

| Phase 1 result | Minus the mansion | Verdict |
|---|---:|---|
| CONSERVATIVE −$53,793 | −$131,989 to −$136,989 | NOT FUNDED |
| **TARGET +$41,317** | **−$36,879 to −$41,879** | **NOT FUNDED** |
| SELLOUT +$136,250 | +$53,054 to +$58,054 | FUNDED |

**Under the old optimistic model the mansion was funded at target. It is not.** Self-funding On The Rocks on ticket revenue alone needs roughly **83% average sell-through**.

So Phase 2 now has exactly three honest routes, and one of them has to be chosen:

1. **Near-sellout across the six months.** ~83% average. Possible, unprecedented.
2. **Land the sponsor layer.** $58,683 covers the $100K gap and materially more than covers the mansion shortfall. It is the only layer where the whole amount is upside rather than execution risk, and it is currently at zero with no deck and no prospect list.
3. **Stage or descope the mansion.** The $68,196 ledger is one weekend at full spec. A smaller first shoot that still produces the sponsor reel is a real option and nobody has priced it.

**This is the decision the six months are actually driving toward, and it should be made in December, not March.**

## The honest read

Nine shows at 70% fill nets about $41,000 over six months. That is a real business built from a negative checking account, and it is **not** $100,000, and it does **not** reach the mansion on its own.

The number is reachable. It needs either a sell-through nobody has achieved yet, or the sponsorship layer to stop being a line item and start being someone's actual job. Saying that in August is worth more than discovering it in February.
