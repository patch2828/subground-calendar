## 🔴 THE PERMIT FINDING THAT ACTUALLY DECIDES IT

**28th Ave is City of Phoenix. The Sept 10 permit was City of Peoria. They are not the same process and the Peoria precedent does not transfer.**

**What Peoria actually did (the real record, not an estimate):**

| | |
|---|---|
| TUP filed | **Mon Aug 10** |
| Plans approved | **Thu Aug 13, 8:36 AM — three days** |
| Fee | $600, paid 8/11, Invoice ACCELA-119946 |
| Event | Thu Sept 10 |
| **Lead time** | **31 days, and it cleared comfortably** |

**What the City of Phoenix publishes:** most events require the application **at least 30 days in advance**, and **large events may require 60 or more days**. A 1,000-plus-capacity bass show on an open lot is a large event by any reading a city planner would apply.

**So Oct 3 in Phoenix sits exactly on the 30-day line with zero margin, on the wrong side of the large-event threshold, with no application started, no site plan, no owner authorization and no insurance bound.** Peoria's three-day turnaround is not a promise Phoenix has made.

That alone ends Oct 3 as an outdoor Phoenix lot date. The Space Laces clash just means it was never worth fighting for anyway.

**Halloween is the one that cannot move.** Oct 31 on a Saturday happens three times in twenty-one years, October is otherwise empty, and it is the largest single swing on the six-month board. Everything else in October is negotiable; that is not.

### The combination worth considering instead

curlyfri321 has a **paid headliner with flights already purchased** and needs a room, promotion and funding. Ryan has **two touring DJs from Corson** who need a room and a date. Those are two halves of one strong bill.

**One October show with DirtySnatcha plus Combine and Northern Road** is a stronger bill than either promoter can put up alone, splits the venue cost across two parties, and gives one date the full weight of both audiences instead of splitting a small market twice. It also turns Ryan from "the guy funding someone else's show for a split" into a co-promoter with talent of his own on the bill, which is a much better position in the settlement conversation.

This is an option, not a recommendation, because it depends on things not yet known: whether curlyfri321 actually controls the artist agreement, and whether Dallas will move the date at all.
