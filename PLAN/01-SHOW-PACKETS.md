- Anything I calculated myself is tagged **ESTIMATE (Claude's model)** with the arithmetic shown so Ryan can check it in ten seconds.
- **Claude never moves money.** Every payment line says Ryan pays it. Claude fills the non-financial fields; Ryan types the digits and presses Confirm.

**The four Pipeline Laws govern every packet**
1. Every event funds the NEXT event's deposits. Show money and life money never touch.
2. Presales are financing. On-sale dates are when the show pays its own bills.
3. Budget to break even on tickets alone at 70% sold. Vendors, merch, sponsors are profit, never survival.
4. Today's $500 vendors are DREAM's department heads.

Plus the DREAM PROJECT law: **Content first. Sponsors second. Festival third.** That is why every packet has a capture plan and no packet ships without one.
