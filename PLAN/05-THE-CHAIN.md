## 3. FINDING TWO — the mansion cannot be booked after the $100K lands

The Gemini blueprint requires a **6-month cascade** from the mansion shoot to the festival: M-6 shoot → M-5 waitlist data trap → M-4 sponsors close and Tier 1 opens → M-3 Tier 2 → M-2/-1 Tier 3 → festival. That is ~183 days and it is not compressible, because the sponsor money and the presale money both come out of footage that does not exist until the shoot happens.

Pepsi Amphitheater's season is May–October and the roadmap targets the **September slot**. Working backward:

| DREAM FEST 1 (Fri–Sun) | Latest possible mansion shoot | Slack after the last show (Feb 20) |
|---|---|---:|
| Sept 3–5, 2027 | Thu Mar 4 | 12 days |
| Sept 10–12, 2027 | Thu Mar 11 | 19 days |
| Sept 17–19, 2027 | Thu Mar 18 | 26 days |
| **Sept 24–26, 2027** | **Thu Mar 25** | **33 days** |

**Even the most generous slot leaves 33 days between the last show and the mansion shoot.** You cannot book two mansions, 30 flights, a five-person film crew, an RF engineer, an IEM rig, lasers with a certified LSO, and catering for 38 people in 33 days, starting from zero, while also closing out a nine-show run.

### So Phase 2 starts inside Phase 1

Mansion Phase 1 is **$6,000 of consultant retainer and pitch-deck design, and the blueprint explicitly says the deck is built BEFORE the shoot with blank slots for the footage.** That is not production spend, it is preparation spend, and it has to happen early.

**Recommended target: DREAM FEST 1 on Sept 24–26, 2027.** It is the latest September Friday-start weekend and it buys the most slack. Working back from it:

| When | What | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Sun Jan 24, 2027** | Mansions, 30 flights, film crew booked | **during the six months** |
| **Mon Feb 8, 2027** | Consultant retained, pitch deck starts ($6,000) | **during the six months** |
| Sat Feb 20, 2027 | Drive-In. Last show of the six months | — |
| **Thu Mar 25, 2027** | Mansion weekend shoots | 33 days after the last show |
| Sun Apr 25, 2027 | Aftermovie drops, presales open | shoot + 31 days |
| **Fri Sep 24 – Sun Sep 26, 2027** | **DREAM FEST 1, Pepsi Amphitheater** | 3 days, camping, 3,000/day |

**January and February 2027 are double-duty months.** You are running the January headline and the February headline and the Drive-In while simultaneously booking a mansion weekend. That is the real shape of the plan and it should be staffed accordingly.

## FINDING FOUR — but the revenue side was under-modeled worse, and that is the good news

The blueprint prices tickets at **$89 / $119 / $149, an average of $125 for a three-day camping festival.** That is regional pricing attached to an Electric Forest build. Published GA ranges for real three-day camping comps:

| Festival | 3-day GA |
|---|---|
| Electric Forest (MI) | $400 – $500 |
| Okeechobee (FL) | $350 – $450 |
| Suwannee Hulaween (FL) | $300 – $400 |
| Sonic Bloom (CO) | $250 – $320 |
| Infrasound (WI) | $250 – $300 |

**Underpricing was the actual risk, not scale.**

| | Blueprint as written | Priced for what is being built |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tickets | $1,250,000 | **$2,579,000** |
| Bar | $400,000 ($40/head) | $650,000 ($65/head) |
| Sponsors | $100,000 | $300,000 |
| Camping / VIP upgrades | **absent** | $250,000 |
| **GROSS** | **$1,750,000** | **$3,779,000** |
| Costs | $970K – $1.8M | $970K – $1.8M |
| **MARGIN** | **−$50,000 to $780,000** | **$1,979,000 to $2,809,000** |

Corrected tiers: 2,000 × $179 early (24h), 4,000 × $229, 3,300 × $279, 700 × $549 VIP.

$65/head at the bar across three days is normal for camping, not aggressive. $300K sponsors is what a built-out content engine sells against EF-level production, and **that is exactly what On The Rocks exists to make possible.** Camping and VIP upgrades were missing from the blueprint's gross entirely, on a festival whose entire premise is camping.

> **The read: at blueprint pricing this festival can lose money at the top of the cost range. Priced correctly, it clears $2M even at the top of the cost range. The vision was never the problem. The price tag on the wristband was.**
