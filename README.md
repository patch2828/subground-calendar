## The four laws every document here obeys

1. Every event funds the **next** event's deposits. Show money and life money never touch.
2. Presales are financing. On-sale dates are when the show pays its own bills.
3. Break even on tickets alone at 70% sold. Vendors, merch and sponsors are profit, never survival.
4. Today's $500 vendors are DREAM's department heads. Pay them on time and they grow at your prices.

Plus: **Content first. Sponsors second. Festival third.**

## What this repo will not do

No fabricated names, prices, links, or stats. No placeholders. No public venue addresses. Nothing published without Ryan's explicit approval. Claude never moves money — every payment line in here says Ryan pays it.

## Ground truth

`PLAN/FACTS.md` is the arbiter. If a document here and FACTS.md disagree, FACTS.md wins and the document is wrong. If FACTS.md and a **live** surface disagree, the live surface wins and FACTS.md needs updating — verify before restating.
